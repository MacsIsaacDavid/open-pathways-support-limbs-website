Our mission

Helping People Live Fully Without Limits

Open Pathways provides education, peer connection, practical resources and community programs for people with limb loss or limb difference. We believe informed choices and human connection can make every step forward feel more possible.

  • Trusted, plain-language information
  • Peer and community connection
  • Resources for families and caregivers
  • Support for accessible, active living
