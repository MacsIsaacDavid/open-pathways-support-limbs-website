# Open Pathways Limb Support Network

Original nonprofit website concept inspired only by the general functionality of limb-loss support organizations. No Amputee Coalition logo, exact branding, exact copy, or proprietary assets are used.

## Included
18+ HTML pages, shared `styles.css`, shared `script.js`, responsive navigation, professional CTA buttons, accessibility features, reduced-motion support, animated statistics, filters, resource search, forms, modal dialogs and donation UI.

## Pages
Home, About Us, Getting Started, Find Support, Resource Center, Support Groups, Peer Support, Youth Programs, Caregiver Resources, Healthcare Navigation, Research, Events, News & Blog, Stories, Advocacy, Volunteer, Donate, Contact, plus News & Events/Get Involved utility pages.

## Production notes
The forms, search, donation flow and social links are UI demos. Connect them to your preferred backend/CMS/payment provider. Replace demo photography with properly licensed or owned images and verify all organizational statistics before publishing.
